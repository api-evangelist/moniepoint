Moniepoint Group CEO Tosin Eniolorunda advocated for layering credit products onto Nigeria's payment infrastructure during the Central Bank's PSV 2028 launch, arguing that transaction data can unlock financing for underserved small businesses. The framework aims to achieve 95% financial inclusion by reaching 50 million more Nigerians with formal banking access.
