Moniepoint hosted an International Women's Day event in partnership with Women Techmakers Lagos and Google Developer Group Lagos, featuring leadership development and hands-on product building to empower women in Nigeria's tech sector. The event included keynote remarks from Kemi Nwogu (Head of Product at Moniepoint Inc), panel discussions, and a buildathon where participants deployed AI-powered solutions.
